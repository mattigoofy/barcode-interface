1. Open **Inventory → Operations → Adjustments → Quick Scan**.
2. Choose **Scrap** or **Add to Stock**, the location and, for a scrap, the
   scrap location and reasons. The next Quick Scan starts with the same choices.
3. Scan a label with a hardware scanner, or fill in **Product**: type a name,
   an internal reference or a barcode. On a phone, the camera button of the
   product field scans the label.
4. The product is shown with its picture, the quantity (1) and the stock in the
   location before and after. Scanning the same product again adds one. Change
   the quantity if needed and press **OK**.

Each booking is listed below the form; **Undo** on a line books the opposite
move. Products tracked by lot/serial number are refused: use the regular Scrap
or Physical Inventory screen for those. Stock is added with the same validated
move an inventory adjustment creates.
