1. Open **Inventory → Operations → Adjustments → Quick Scan**.
2. Choose **Scrap** or **Add to Stock**, the location and, for a scrap, the
   scrap location and reasons. The choices are remembered on the device.
3. Scan: with a hardware scanner, by typing the barcode (or internal reference)
   and pressing Enter, or with the camera button (HTTPS or localhost).

When there is not enough stock to scrap, the screen asks whether to scrap
anyway. Products tracked by lot/serial number are refused: use the regular
Scrap or Physical Inventory screen for those. Stock is added with the same
validated move an inventory adjustment creates.
